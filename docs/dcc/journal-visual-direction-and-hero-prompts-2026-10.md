**Shared visual grammar:** artist-run cultural infrastructure expressed through one precise physical metaphor, modest objects and believable wear, dark ink petrol-blue, small deep teal/cyan highlights and limited warm clay/amber illumination. Contemplative, tactile, cinematic editorial still life; slightly uncanny but materially plausible. Wide landscape 16:9 at 1672×941 or larger matching proportion. Deep shadows with central focal subject, clean negative space and thumbnail readability. No generated typography, logos, fake numerical readouts or people posed as real DCC participants; reject neon cyberpunk cities, glowing stock network graphics, giant immaculate labs and generic SaaS interfaces. If the composition differs from existing reference covers, defer to the actual image references after visual review.

Generated images must never be presented as proof of a real project. Studio 43 article should preferentially use an actual licensed/approved photograph; generated alternative must be credited as a conceptual illustration.

## 01. The Institution That Can Build Itself — hero

**Concept:** A tiny institution constructed from functions rather than a monumental building.

**PROMPT:** Cinematic editorial still life of an improvised, working tabletop institution inside a modest artist-run studio. Three small modular steel racks stand on one scarred worktable; blank archival folders, clamps, a prototype, a closed laptop and a few carefully routed physical cables link separate components, as if a community has assembled a useful shared structure from available parts. One rack can be removed and replaced without collapsing everything. Human-scale and credible, quietly sculptural, gently paradoxical. Dominant deep petrol-black shadows (#02171B), teal/cyan edge light (#056C78), one warm amber-orange task light (#E18B60), subtle photographic grain and true material wear; low cinematic angle, carefully preserved negative space and strong thumbnail silhouette. No people, text, logo, floating holograms, giant data center or sleek executive architecture. Landscape 16:9, 1672×941.

Alt: Modular worktable structure of tools, archive objects and physical connections, suggesting a community-maintained institution.

## 02. You Can't Buy Digital Culture — hero

**Concept:** A stack of things you can buy next to the one thing you cannot buy.

**PROMPT:** An artist-led conceptual editorial photograph: an awkward precarious tower of ordinary anonymous blank product boxes and generic small electronic devices on a worn studio table, several neat cords plugged into nothing. In front, one small unfinished translucent handmade object sits in a warm pool of light, completely separate from the expensive-looking but useless stack. Quiet dry humor, imperfect material realism, strong contrast of accumulation against actual creative practice. Dark blue-black surroundings at roughly 80 percent tonal weight, restrained teal-cyan reflection on boxes and one contained amber highlight on the object, rich texture, soft film grain, gallery-caliber but not advertising. Uncluttered 16:9 landscape cover, 1672×941, striking silhouette at thumbnail scale. No text, brands, logo, interface, invented people, money, digital neon city or inspirational corporate stock imagery.

Alt: Tower of blank product boxes and unplugged devices contrasted with one unfinished handmade object.

## 03. Building Node 001: Studio 43 — hero alternative

**Concept:** A single working node whose usefulness extends beyond the frame. Preferred source: actual Studio 43 photography.

**PROMPT:** A conceptual cinematic image of a compact independent artist's fabrication workbench, explicitly NOT pretending to reproduce any real studio interior: a desktop 3D printer partially cropped at one edge, an unfinished printed object resting on a scarred table, calipers, one filament spool, everyday cords crossing the table toward an unseen workspace outside the right edge. One specific workbench, not a factory. Familiar practical scale, believable plastic and steel, honest wear. Very dark petrol-blue interior with restrained cool cyan machine spill and a warm burnt-orange practical lamp; directional editorial light, deliberate negative space, 16:9 at 1672×941. No people, DCC or Bakehouse signage, fictional room number, fabricated actual members, typography, fake lab dashboards, recognizable false brand details or glowing network overlay.

Alt: Conceptual small fabrication workbench with desktop printer, unfinished prototype and cables leading outside the frame.
