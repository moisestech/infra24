# DCC Journal — Next Essay Cover Prompts & Supporting Images

See companion file: docs/dcc/journal-visual-direction-and-hero-prompts-2026-10.md. These are briefs for generated **conceptual editorial illustrations**, not documentary photos.

Common direction: 16:9 landscape 1672×941, roughly 80% deep petrol blue-black, muted cyan/teal highlights, sparse warm clay-orange practical light. Low-budget believable studio materiality, subtle film grain, a single poetic physical metaphor, high thumbnail contrast, negative space. No text, numerals, logos, glowing corporate network graphics, false identities or fictitious DCC events.

## 04. Capacity Exists Before Infrastructure Does — hero

**Concept:** Available equipment is not the same as dependable service. The image should contain a promise not yet made.

**PROMPT:** Photographic editorial tableau inside a dark compact fabrication workshop. Three separate islands of real everyday equipment: a dormant desktop 3D printer with a filament spool on the left, an unfinished translucent printed part alone at center, and a small inspection station with calipers, safety glasses and a warm task lamp on the right. A single loose cable runs across the table but ends unplugged just short of the inspection station. No person present to authorize or finish the handoff. Precise, materially plausible and faintly absurd; the visible missing connection is the central subject. Deep petrol-black room (#031518), teal cyan machine-edge reflections (#67C6CE), restrained orange task light (#E18B60), subtle grain, cinematic but honest and low budget. 16:9 landscape 1672×941, quiet negative space, no text, prices, fake jobs, invented shop branding or diagram overlay.

Alt: Unused fabrication equipment and an unfinished object sit between two workstations, with one physical connection left incomplete.

## 05. An Invitation to Build DCC Miami With Us — hero

**Concept:** Belonging represented by room made for an unfinished contribution, not a staged group photo.

**PROMPT:** An intimate conceptual editorial photograph of a very modest artist-run creative technology studio after hours. Three mismatched work chairs around a small scarred worktable; one chair is pulled back inviting someone to sit. On the table are an unfinished translucent 3D-printed sculptural fragment, a plain pencil, a metal tool, and a single warm practical lamp. In the distance, a dark open doorway suggests other rooms and people beyond the frame, but no person appears. Believable independent Miami-scale studio without a landmark, not a white cube or generic coworking office. Cool almost-black petrol blue environment with subtle cyan reflective accent and a generous but contained clay-orange lamp glow; human warmth inside a consistent DCC dark palette. Natural imperfection, soft grain, editorial photography, clear silhouette and spacious composition in 16:9 1672×941. No faces, staged crowds, handshakes, logos, legible words, invented event, sales cues or floating digital network.

Alt: An open chair beside an unfinished artwork and a softly lit studio table suggests an invitation to collaborate.

## Asset handling

Use art-approved source/reference covers rather than attempting to guess the original cover compositions from color metadata alone. Export hero landscape PNG or WebP in 16:9; keep nontext composition. For actual Studio 43, use real photographs if possible. Generated imagery must be described as conceptual in captions/metadata. Suggested Cloudinary target: dccmiami/journal/2026-10/. Do not invent Cloudinary URLs: create media, upload, verify returned URL, then update heroImage and heroImageAlt in editorial.ts. Supporting images need approved image files and an explicit source before an EditorialImageSlot is given a src.
