# DCC Era Pathways

Born-Digital Era pathways connect network, workshops, clinics, open lab, public interfaces, and newsletter into one field map.

## Pathways (summary)

1. **Network** — join the Index  
2. **Workshops** — structured learning  
3. **Clinics** — short practical sessions  
4. **Open Lab** — drop-in studio  
5. **Public interfaces** — websites, signage, QR  
6. **Newsletter** — recurring digest  

## Asking about pathways

Staff and public visitors can ask how to get started. Prefer citing this doc and the network overview when explaining onboarding order.
