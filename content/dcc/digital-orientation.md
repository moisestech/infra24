# DCC Digital Orientation

A digital orientation statement is a short paragraph describing how someone works with digital tools, media, or code.

## Why it matters

Network readiness scoring and workshop matching use orientation text plus practice tags. Incomplete orientations keep contacts below the MVP readiness threshold.

## Good orientation examples

- “I build browser-based installations and teach vibecoding clinics.”
- “I document artist websites and help studios adopt ethical AI workflows.”

## Privacy

Orientation text used in public answers must be approved-for-public-AI. Do-not-use-in-AI records are excluded from Memory Agent retrieval.
