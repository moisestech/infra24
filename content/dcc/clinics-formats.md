# DCC Clinics — Formats

## Clinic vs workshop

Clinics are shorter, drop-in friendly sessions. Workshops are multi-hour intensives with clearer prerequisites and deliverables.

## Typical formats

- **Demo + make** — short tool demo followed by a build block
- **Critique** — share work-in-progress and structured feedback
- **Office hours** — open help for websites, documentation, and AI literacy

## Themes that recur

AI literacy, vibecoding, net art, documentation, portfolio readiness, and digital presence for artists.

## How to find sessions

Ask the Institutional Memory agent for upcoming clinics matching a theme, or browse published programming records on the DCC site.
