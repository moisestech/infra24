# DCC Network Signup — FAQ

## What is the DCC Index?

A map of people working in Miami digital culture. Your profile helps DCC match you to workshops, partners, and opportunities.

## What fields matter for network readiness?

Email, practice tags, a short digital orientation statement, social or portfolio link, and consent to contact. Staff scoring uses a 100-point readiness model; 70% is the MVP threshold for “network ready.”

## What happens after signup?

Staff may propose follow-ups: invite to complete your profile, invite to a workshop, or draft a partner introduction. Proposed actions go to an Agent Approvals queue—nothing sends automatically in the pilot.

## Privacy and AI use

Records marked do-not-use-in-AI are excluded from the Institutional Memory agent. Public mode only uses approved-for-public-AI fields.

## Workshops vs network membership

Signing up for the index is not the same as registering for a paid workshop. Workshop registration links appear on published programming records when available.

## Contact

For pilot questions, contact DCC staff through the channels listed on the public network page.
