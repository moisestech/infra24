# DCC Partner Pathways

DCC partners include galleries, labs, schools, and civic programs that host workshops, exhibitions, and shared infrastructure.

## What partners get

Shared workshop catalog listings, optional network-index invitations for their communities, and pilot access to governed institutional memory tools for staff Q&A.

## Partner readiness

Partners should provide a public contact, program description, and consent for how DCC may list their offerings. Network readiness scoring may flag partners with incomplete profiles for staff follow-up.

## Approvals

Outreach drafts proposed by the Network Readiness Agent are written to an Agent Approvals queue. Humans approve before any public message is sent.
