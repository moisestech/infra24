# DCC Artist Support

Artist support includes digital audits, documentation help, portfolio readiness, and presentation support for public opportunities.

## Digital audits

Light-touch reviews of websites, link hygiene, and documentation gaps—not full agency redesigns.

## Portfolio readiness

Help framing work for open calls, residencies, and partner shows. May cross-link to workshop themes (documentation, digital presence).

## How Memory Agent helps

Staff can ask which support modules exist and how they differ from workshops. Cite DCC docs; do not invent grant amounts or eligibility unless in CRM records.
