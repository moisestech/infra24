# DCC Workshop Catalog (Pilot)

## AI and net art

Workshops for artists exploring generative tools, the browser as medium, and networked practice. Suitable for studio artists moving into web-native or AI-assisted workflows.

## Vibecoding and creative code

Practical sessions on building small web experiences with AI-assisted coding. Emphasis on voice, constraints, evaluation criteria, and publishing—not hype.

## AI literacy for creators

Clinics on prompt design, documentation, rights awareness, and when *not* to use AI in studio practice. Aligns with DCC’s ethical framing for digital citizenship.

## Video and performance

Sessions on voice, movement, and live performance with digital tools—often cross-listed with partner organizations.

## Signup and pathways

New participants can join via the DCC network signup flow. Pathways include artist, educator, partner, and technologist tracks. Staff use readiness scoring to invite index completion and workshop fit.

## Booking and public programming

Public workshops and partner events appear in programming records when published. Ask the Institutional Memory agent for upcoming workshops matching themes like AI, net art, or digital culture.
