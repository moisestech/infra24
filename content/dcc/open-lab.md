# DCC Open Lab

Open Lab is a low-friction physical entry point—drop-in studio time where artists can work, ask questions, and meet the network.

## What to expect

Shared tables, wifi, occasional facilitator support, and pointers into workshops or the DCC Index. Not a certified makerspace or fabrication shop by default.

## How Open Lab relates to the Index

Visitors can join the Index later. Staff may invite frequent Open Lab participants to complete profiles when readiness fields are missing.

## Asking the agent

Staff can ask what Open Lab covers this month or how it differs from booked workshops. Answers should cite programming or DCC docs when available.
