# Digital Culture Center Miami — Network Overview

The Digital Culture Center (DCC) connects artists, creative technologists, educators, and partners working at the intersection of art, software, and digital culture in Miami.

## Mission

DCC builds practical infrastructure for Miami’s digital culture network: workshops, the DCC Index (people map), partner programming, and pilot tools that help institutions publish and govern public-facing knowledge.

## Network Index

The DCC Index is a living map of people and practices. Members share practice tags, digital orientation statements, and consent for contact. Network readiness scoring helps staff prioritize outreach and invitations.

## Workshops and clinics

DCC runs hands-on clinics for artists working with AI, the web, video, and creative code. Topics include net art, vibecoding, AI literacy, and ethical use of generative tools. Workshops are designed for practitioners who want to ship work—not only theory.

## Institutional memory pilot

The Institutional Memory agent lets staff ask questions about programming, people, and network context using governed Airtable and Supabase data. Public outputs require approval before QR handoff or signage.

## Who should join

Artists, creative technologists, educators, curators, and partners interested in Miami digital culture, open workshops, and collaborative infrastructure.
